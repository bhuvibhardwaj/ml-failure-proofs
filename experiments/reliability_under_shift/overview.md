# Reliability Under Shift

This research track investigates how machine learning systems exhibit hidden failure behavior under changing input conditions.

Core themes explored:
- Accuracy as a lagging reliability signal
- Confidence drift under covariate shift
- Hidden instability in aggregate metrics
- Calibration failure
- Internal representation degradation
- Failure propagation through neural networks

The experiments progressively examine how models can appear stable during validation while silently degrading under deployment-like conditions.

Research Direction:
Toward mechanistic detection of reliability degradation before observable accuracy collapse.
